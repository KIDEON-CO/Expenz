Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 15, 2026

Expenz ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Expenz app.

1. Information We Collect

Expenz is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit your personal data to any servers. The only data stored is what you enter in the app, such as expenses, custom categories, notes, and budget limits.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used solely to provide the functionality of the app: calculating totals, displaying charts, and handling CSV exports. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or marketing purposes.

3. Pro Features

If you choose to upgrade to Expenz Pro, any purchase information is processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not store or have access to your payment information.

4. Data Sharing & Security

Expenz does not share your data with third parties. Any export is controlled entirely by you. Your data is protected by your device's security features.

5. Children

Expenz is suitable for all ages. We do not collect or transmit any personal information from children, giving parents full control.
